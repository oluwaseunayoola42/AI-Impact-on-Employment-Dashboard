# AI Impact on Employment Dashboard (2026–2030)

An Excel-based data analytics project exploring how artificial intelligence is projected to reshape global employment between 2026 and 2030. The dashboard shows which roles are most at risk, which industries face the most disruption, where hiring is strongest, and how education level relates to salary.

![Dashboard preview](images/dashboard.png)

---

## Objective

To give workforce planners, job seekers, and recruiters a clear, data-driven view of:

- Which job roles have the highest future demand and the highest automation risk
- Which industries are most exposed to AI replication
- How 2026 hiring trends differ across countries
- How education level relates to average salary

## Tools

- **Microsoft Excel**: data cleaning, preparation, analysis, and interactive dashboard design

## Dataset

**File:** `AI_Impact_on_Jobs_2030.xlsx`

The dataset covers projected global employment and AI automation trends from 2026 to 2030:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Job title | Role name, with a future demand score |
| Automation risk | AI automation risk score by role |
| Country | 2026 hiring trend across Australia, Canada, Germany, India, Japan, Pakistan, Singapore, UAE, UK, and USA |
| Industry | AI replication risk score for Education, Energy, Finance, Government, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Media, Retail, Technology, and Transportation |
| Education level | Average salary for High School, Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD |

> Scores are used as provided in the source dataset.

## Dashboard Panels

1. **Job Title by Future Demand Score**: horizontal bar chart ranking roles by projected demand
2. **Country Hiring Trend 2026**: column chart comparing open roles across 10 countries
3. **AI Replication Risk by Industry**: bar chart showing the most vulnerable sectors
4. **AI Automation Risk by Job Title (Top 10)**: ranking of the most at-risk roles
5. **Average Salary by Education Level**: earning potential across qualification levels

## Key Insights

1. **Software Developer leads future demand** with a score of 102.89, followed by Cybersecurity Analyst (99.9) and Product Manager (97.69). These roles combine technical depth with human judgement.
2. **Government and Energy face the highest AI replication risk**, scoring 170.8 and 167.22 respectively. This points to significant restructuring ahead in public services and utilities.
3. **Hiring is strongest in Singapore and the USA.** Singapore recorded the most open roles in 2026 (347), reflecting strong demand for tech and analytics talent.

## Recommendations

1. **Upskill in AI-adjacent roles.** Prompt Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and Robotics Engineer show strong future demand. Invest in skills that complement AI rather than compete with it.
2. **Target high-hiring markets.** Singapore, the USA, and the UAE show the strongest 2026 hiring. Job seekers and recruiters should prioritise talent pipelines there.
3. **Reskill in Government and Energy.** With the highest replication risk, organisations in these sectors need proactive workforce transition plans to retain institutional knowledge.
4. **Align education with industry needs.** The salary data suggests a Master's degree paired with practical tools (SQL, Power BI, Python) delivers the strongest return. Pair academic study with portfolio projects and certifications.
